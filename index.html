              Glossary

              One name per figure. Use the name in the first column in anything issued; the other names are listed so a figure quoted elsewhere can be traced back.

              Other reported metrics

              Reported with every area schedule, beside the categories. The calculator cells below are linked to the efficiency example in Efficiency calculations, so IPMS 1, Sellable and Total Gross Building Area flow through. Numbers loaded are illustrative.

              Unit blocks, keys and seats

              Block the Sellable area by unit type, with the number of units in each block, to get the average area per unit type. Enter seats for F&B outlets. The check line flags any difference between the blocks and the Sellable line in the calculator.

              Envelope perimeter

              • Measure in plan along the outer face of the external walls that enclose Interior AC and BOH: the IPMS 1 boundary. One figure per floor.
              • Exclude balustrades and upstands of Exterior Covered, and detached structures (report those on their own line).
              • Report in m first, ft second, to the nearest 0.1 m.
              • Used for facade cost and for the Waller envelope efficiency (reference only): perfect perimeter (4 × √IPMS 1 of the floor) ÷ actual perimeter.

              Floor-to-floor heights

              • Measure from the top of structural slab to the top of the structural slab above, for each level. For the top level, measure to the top of the roof structure.
              • Report per level in m to 0.01, and in ft-in, beside the level's area.
              • Gross facade area per level = envelope perimeter × floor-to-floor height. Facade to floor ratio = gross facade area ÷ IPMS 1.
