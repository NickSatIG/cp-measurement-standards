# Costa Palmas Measurement Standards: Change Log

Prepared for Irongate. Newest revision first. Each revision is also summarized in the page footer.

## Rev. 4, 16 October 2026

- **Branded Home typology:** the schematic blocking diagram is replaced with the CP Branded Home area plan (area schedule Rev. 1), coloured with the standard category swatches. The plan zooms on hover and opens an enlarged view on click, with a full-size option.
- **Branded Home figures:** the table now uses the example's real numbers:
  - Sellable 512.2 m², BOH (EXT) 96.2 m², Covered Sellable 89.0 m²
  - IPMS 1 608.5 m², Total Gross Buildable Area 697.5 m²
  - Pool shell / water surface 120.6 / 104.1 m²
  - Development efficiency 86.2%, construction efficiency 84.2% enclosed and 86.2% covered
- **Typology table:** the Branded Home table gains site-area lines, not added to the gross: Exterior Uncovered 293.7 m² and Landscape Planting 1,377.0 m².
- **Waller envelope efficiency:** on the Branded Home card it is now computed per building. The Main House shows 72.0%.
- **Number display:** every figure on the page rounds to one decimal place.
- **Efficiency calculator:**
  - Renamed "Efficiency Example".
  - The three headline ratios show as large figures in the header, so they are visible while the line items are collapsed.
  - The small one-line summary is removed, and a chevron icon shows whether the section is open or closed.
- **Efficiency metrics section removed:**
  - The three ratio definitions moved to "More info" pop-ups in Efficiency Calculations.
  - The CP Homes envelope efficiency became step 8 under "How it is calculated".
  - The Reconciling line (enclosure delta) block was removed. Enclosure delta stays in the calculator, in step 7 and in the glossary.
- **Category cards:** "How it is calculated" and "International references" (the IPMS, BOMA, Superficie construida and USALI equivalents, plus the links to each standard) are now collapsed sections on every category card and on the Total Gross Buildable Area card.
- **Navigation:** a "How to use this page" link is added at the top of the side nav.
- **Yacht Club typology (added 19 October 2026):** the schematic blocking diagram is replaced with a Yacht Club blocking diagram coloured by area category and read from the concept plan, with the same hover zoom and click to enlarge as Branded Home.
- **Yacht Club figures (added 19 October 2026):** the table now uses the concept area totals, converted from ft²:
  - FOH Non-Revenue 328.9 m² (3,060 ft² plus the 480 ft² Kid's Club), BOH (INT) 158.9 m², BOH (EXT) 132.9 m², Covered Non-Revenue 221.1 m²
  - IPMS 1 620.6 m², Total Gross Buildable Area 841.7 m²
  - Pool Area 319.6 m²; site areas: Exterior Uncovered 1,012.6 m², Landscape Planting 766.4 m², Road 307.5 m²
  - Construction efficiency 53.0% enclosed and 65.3% covered (headline); development efficiency 0%, not meaningful
- **Typography (added 21 October 2026):** the brand faces (Sharp Roman, Sharp Serif, Founders Grotesk, Pitch) are replaced on screen with Source Serif 4 for headings, IBM Plex Sans for body text, labels and tables, and IBM Plex Mono for figures, for legibility. This is a deliberate deviation from the CP brand typography.
- **Condo building typology (added 22 October 2026):** the schematic blocking diagram is replaced with the Marina Cove Resi M13 Level 1 area plan, with hover zoom and click to enlarge. The table uses the totals from the Marina Cove Resi M13 area schedule, Rev. 0:
  - Sellable 1,705.8 m², BOH (INT) 40.5 m², BOH (EXT) 7.4 m², Covered Sellable 298.0 m², Covered Non-Revenue 31.0 m² (2.8 m² of it covered service)
  - IPMS 1 1,753.7 m², Total Gross Buildable Area 2,082.8 m²
  - Pool shell 70.4 m²; site areas: Exterior Uncovered 273.6 m², Landscape Planting 93.1 m²
  - Development efficiency 96.2% (headline); construction efficiency 97.3% enclosed and 97.6% covered
- **Section renamed (22 October 2026):** "Efficiency by typology" is now "Examples per Typology".

## Rev. 3, 15 October 2026

- Exterior Covered sub-lines renamed Covered Sellable and Covered Non-Revenue (Rev. 1 and 2 called them Revenue-tied and Public).
- Interior AC gains a third sub-line, Circulation: corridors, lift lobbies, guest stairs and guest lifts, which move from BOH. Service lifts stay in BOH.
- Construction efficiency now uses occupiable area (Sellable + FOH Non-Revenue) as its numerator:
  - Enclosed basis: over IPMS 1.
  - Covered basis: over Total Gross Buildable Area.
  - Covered service areas stay in the gross but are not occupiable.
- Development efficiency is unchanged, with a note that balconies count.
- Gross confirmed as the controlling figure, with net derived from it.
- Calculator made collapsible.
- Open items removed.
- Audience stated.
- Typology examples titled for Gulf Villa, Yacht Club, Casa Blake Phase 2 and a condo building, still illustrative.

## Rev. 2, 15 October 2026

- Glossary added, listing the names each figure goes by.
- Enclosure delta (IPMS 1 − IPMS 2) added as a reconciling line for teams that measure Interior AC to the finish face, in the efficiency metrics and the calculator.
- Other Reported Metrics section added after the area categories, linked to the calculator: unit blocks, keys and seats; envelope perimeter; floor-to-floor heights.
- Open items table added.
- Area schedule deliverable checklist added.
- BOH split into two sub-lines, both enclosed and both counted in Total Gross Buildable Area: BOH (INT): Conditioned and BOH (EXT): Unconditioned. This replaces the Rev. 1 meaning of BOH (EXT).
- Roofed but unenclosed service areas moved to Exterior Covered: Public. Open service yards and pads moved to Exterior Uncovered.
- No ratio definition changed. Rev. 1 is kept so it can be restored.

## Rev. 1, 14 October 2026

- Interior AC and Exterior Covered split into revenue and non-revenue sub-lines.
- Pool Area moved under Building Area, outside the gross total, and measured to the pool shell as well as the water surface.
- Efficiency rewritten as development and construction efficiency, with a worked example and typology examples.
- Waller envelope efficiency added for reference.
- Vertical and horizontal scope defined.
- CP column added to the standard-by-purpose table.
- Reporting protocol updated for sub-categories, unit blocks and cost split.
