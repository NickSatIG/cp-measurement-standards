# Costa Palmas Measurement Standards: Change Log

Prepared for Irongate. Newest revision first. Each revision is also summarized in the page footer.

## Rev. 6, 23 October 2026

- **Name change:** "Total Gross Buildable Area" is renamed "Total Gross Building Area" everywhere on the page, including this change log. (Spanish: "Área bruta total de edificación".)
- **Language toggle:** an English / Español (MX) switch at the top of the page translates the whole page, including category names, the calculator, pop-ups and the footer. The choice is remembered per viewer.
- **Header subtitle:** now reads "What each area schedule includes and how it is measured."
- **Category diagram:** each box shows a short description on hover. The efficiency diagram flashes when it redraws from the Efficiency example, and its "How it is calculated" list is now a collapsed dropdown.
- **Condo building plan:** the Marina Cove Resi M13 Level 1 diagram is replaced with an updated version showing the unit floor plans under the area colours.
- **Hotel plan:** the schematic blocking diagram on the Hotel tab is replaced with the Casa Blake Phase 2 blocking diagram coloured by area category, with hover zoom and click to enlarge. Its table figures are still illustrative.
- **Efficiency diagram caption:** the two opening sentences are removed; the caption keeps the key to the bars and the live-update note.

## Rev. 5, 22 October 2026

- **Page layout:**
  - "How to use this page" is cut to one paragraph, including the units sentence (m² first, ft² second, converted once at the end). The bullets on figure labelling, the controlling model, never merging exterior area, vertical and horizontal scope, gross controls with the IPMS 2 formula, and audience are removed.
  - The F&B outlet plan moves into "How the categories add up". The separate "One outlet, split by category" section is removed, and the "Open terrace is site area" note is dropped from the plan's legend.
  - Total Gross Building Area moves directly before Efficiency Calculations.
  - "Efficiency by typology" is renamed "Examples per Typology", loses its intro paragraph, and becomes the first Reference section.
  - Other Reported Metrics moves to the end of the Reference section, after the Glossary.
- **Sidebar:** a top group, above Building area, lists How to use this page, How the categories add up, Total Gross Building Area and Efficiency calculations. Examples per typology heads the Reference group and Other reported metrics closes it. Every sidebar label now matches its heading exactly.
- **Headings:** every main section uses the same heading level, with category, typology and metric cards one level below. Headings are in sentence case. The duplicate title on the Total Gross Building Area card is removed. The efficiency example now sits under the Efficiency calculations heading, ahead of the diagram.
- **Typology tabs:**
  - "Yacht Club" is renamed "Amenity Building" and "Casa Blake Phase 2" is renamed "Hotel". Each source project is still named in the card.
  - The tag boxes and dashed swatch are removed from each tab heading.
- **Amenity Building typology (Yacht Club):** the schematic blocking diagram is replaced with a Yacht Club blocking diagram coloured by area category and read from the concept plan, with the same hover zoom and click to enlarge as Branded Home.
- **Amenity Building figures (Yacht Club):** the table now uses the concept area totals, converted from ft²:

- **Typography:** the brand faces (Sharp Roman, Sharp Serif, Founders Grotesk, Pitch) are replaced on screen with Source Serif 4 for headings, IBM Plex Sans for body text, labels and tables, and IBM Plex Mono for figures, for legibility. This is a deliberate deviation from the CP brand typography.

## Rev. 4, 16 October 2026

- **Branded Home typology:** the schematic blocking diagram is replaced with the CP Branded Home area plan (area schedule Rev. 1), coloured with the standard category swatches. The plan zooms on hover and opens an enlarged view on click, with a full-size option.
- **Branded Home figures:** the table now uses the example's real numbers:

- **Waller envelope efficiency:** on the Branded Home card it is now computed per building. The Main House shows 72.0%.
- **Number display:** every figure on the page rounds to one decimal place.
- **Efficiency calculator:**
  - Renamed "Efficiency Example".
  - The three headline ratios show as large figures in the header, so they are visible while the line items are collapsed.
  - The small one-line summary is removed, and a chevron icon shows whether the section is open or closed.
- **Efficiency metrics section removed:**
  - The three ratio definitions moved to "More info" pop-ups in Efficiency Calculations.
  - The CP Homes envelope efficiency became step 8 under "How it is calculated".
  - The Reconciling line (enclosure delta) block was removed. Enclosure delta stays in the calculator, in step 7 and in the glossary.
- **Category cards:** "How it is calculated" and "International references" (the IPMS, BOMA, Superficie construida and USALI equivalents, plus the links to each standard) are now collapsed sections on every category card and on the Total Gross Building Area card.
- **Navigation:** a "How to use this page" link is added at the top of the side nav.

## Rev. 3, 15 October 2026

- Exterior Covered sub-lines renamed Covered Sellable and Covered Non-Revenue (Rev. 1 and 2 called them Revenue-tied and Public).
- Interior AC gains a third sub-line, Circulation: corridors, lift lobbies, guest stairs and guest lifts, which move from BOH. Service lifts stay in BOH.
- Construction efficiency now uses occupiable area (Sellable + FOH Non-Revenue) as its numerator:
  - Enclosed basis: over IPMS 1.
  - Covered basis: over Total Gross Building Area.
  - Covered service areas stay in the gross but are not occupiable.
- Development efficiency is unchanged, with a note that balconies count.
- Gross confirmed as the controlling figure, with net derived from it.
- Calculator made collapsible.
- Open items removed.
- Audience stated.
- Typology examples titled for Gulf Villa, Yacht Club, Casa Blake Phase 2 and a condo building, still illustrative.

## Rev. 2, 15 October 2026

- Glossary added, listing the names each figure goes by.
- Enclosure delta (IPMS 1 − IPMS 2) added as a reconciling line for teams that measure Interior AC to the finish face, in the efficiency metrics and the calculator.
- Other Reported Metrics section added after the area categories, linked to the calculator: unit blocks, keys and seats; envelope perimeter; floor-to-floor heights.
- Open items table added.
- Area schedule deliverable checklist added.
- BOH split into two sub-lines, both enclosed and both counted in Total Gross Building Area: BOH (INT): Conditioned and BOH (EXT): Unconditioned. This replaces the Rev. 1 meaning of BOH (EXT).
- Roofed but unenclosed service areas moved to Exterior Covered: Public. Open service yards and pads moved to Exterior Uncovered.
- No ratio definition changed. Rev. 1 is kept so it can be restored.

## Rev. 1, 14 October 2026

- Interior AC and Exterior Covered split into revenue and non-revenue sub-lines.
- Pool Area moved under Building Area, outside the gross total, and measured to the pool shell as well as the water surface.
- Efficiency rewritten as development and construction efficiency, with a worked example and typology examples.
- Waller envelope efficiency added for reference.
- Vertical and horizontal scope defined.
- CP column added to the standard-by-purpose table.
- Reporting protocol updated for sub-categories, unit blocks and cost split.
